Questions about FlutterFlow

The specifics people check before they build: code, the web, sign-in, payments and the stores.

Can I export my app’s code?

No. FlutterFlow builds and changes your app for you, and you can download the built phone files (.apk, .aab and .ipa) from Releases. If you want the code, build in FlutterFlow Studio.

Can I put my app on the web?

Through a share link: it opens in any browser without signing in, and visitors can add it to their Home Screen. Custom domains aren’t available yet.

Which sign-in methods can my app use?

Email and password, and guest accounts. Google and Apple sign-in aren’t available yet.

Can my app take payments?

Yes. FlutterFlow can help you build one-off payments and recurring subscriptions through Stripe Checkout. App Store and Google Play purchases aren’t available yet.

Do I need developer accounts to publish?

Yes. FlutterFlow connects to your own accounts and sends each version through.
