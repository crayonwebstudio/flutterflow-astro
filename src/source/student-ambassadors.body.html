FlutterFlow Student Ambassadors

FlutterFlow Student Ambassadors are university students who want to help other students learn about FlutterFlow.

Become a Student Ambassador (opens in a new tab)

These are the types of events you could host

  • Meetups

    Informal gatherings for networking, sharing experiences, and discussing the latest FlutterFlow trends.

  • Workshops

    Expert-led hands-on sessions to deepen understanding through practical exercises and real-world examples.

  • Hackathons

    Competitive events where teams create innovative solutions using FlutterFlow within a limited time frame, fostering creativity and teamwork.

FAQs

Who can be a FlutterFlow Student Ambassador?

Enrolled university students (undergraduate and graduate) and people who agree to the terms and conditions can apply to be a FlutterFlow Student Ambassador on their university campus. Full-time and part-time enrollment are both fine.

Terms and conditions (opens in a new tab)
What would I be doing?

You would speak at club events (entrepreneurship, computer science, or other relevant clubs) and/or host events about FlutterFlow about every 2 months.

What are the advantages of being a FlutterFlow Student Ambassador?

Network: Connect with like-minded FlutterFlow developers, other app developers, and FlutterFlow staff. Access: Receive invites to FlutterFlow events. Experience: Gain leadership experience.
