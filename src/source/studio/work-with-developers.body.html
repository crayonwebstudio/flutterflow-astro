Our Experts can help with everything from advising on an integration to full end-to-end app development; custom code to design systems. Check out their profiles to see services offered!

Each developer or agency is verified by FlutterFlow, and many hold the coveted FlutterFlow Studio Expert badge, proving advanced skills and hands-on experience with our platform.

While all developers in our program have been evaluated for proficiency in FlutterFlow Studio, the FlutterFlow Studio Experts badge is awarded to freelancers and agencies that have successfully completed our in-house training course to further their proficiency in FlutterFlow Studio.

Expert Program FAQ

How can I find the right FlutterFlow Studio Agency or Freelancer for my project?

We offer two ways to find our developers.

The Partner Portal: Our Partner Portal is managed by our in-house team and is home to all of our available developers. You can browse & filter by your selection criteria (like budget, location, language, services), or create a project proposal and get a curated selection of Experts who are the best fit for your project and preferences.

Contra: If you prefer a marketplace, a number of our developers are available on Contra. Browse by location, budget, certification, view past work, and create an account to reach out!

What should I include in my Project Proposal?

Filling out the Project Proposal only takes a few minutes and allows you to send requests to multiple Experts at once instead of sending individual requests. While some questions are optional, we recommend including key project details and providing as much context as possible so you can be matched with an ideal partner. Don’t worry about being 100% certain about everything — you can always adjust project requests, timeline requirements, and budget once you’ve connected with Experts. Be honest about your budget restrictions, but try not to limit yourself to only the lowest budget option at the matchmaking stage, as it will limit the number of eligible Experts.

What does an “Expert” badge mean?

Experts are agencies or freelancers that FlutterFlow has evaluated as having a high degree of technical proficiency with building apps in FlutterFlow Studio. To become an Expert, agencies or freelancers must go through an application and review process with FlutterFlow where FlutterFlow reviews their portfolio of work and requires them to pass FlutterFlow’s Expert level training. When hiring an Expert, customers are recommended to do their own due diligence with Experts around other aspects of client work like on-time delivery, IP rights etc. If needed, we suggest getting a contract in place with the Expert to ensure all your project goals are met.

Who are FlutterFlow Studio Agencies?

FlutterFlow Studio agencies have been verified by FlutterFlow as agencies having the technical skills to build with FlutterFlow Studio. Agencies go through an application and review process with FlutterFlow before being listed here. When hiring an Agency, customers are recommended to do their own due diligence with Agencies around other aspects of client work like on-time delivery, IP rights etc. If needed, we suggest getting a contract in place with the Expert to ensure all your project goals are met.
