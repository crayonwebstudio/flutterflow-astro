Questions about plans

One subscription, two ways to build. How plans, usage and seats work across them.

Does one plan cover FlutterFlow and FlutterFlow Studio?

Yes. One subscription covers FlutterFlow and FlutterFlow Studio. Each plan has the same name in both.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Pay yearly and get a discount. Switch the plans above to Yearly to see what each plan costs.

How does AI usage work?

FlutterFlow and Studio share one allowance, shown as the percentage left and when it resets. On Pro and Max you can buy a refill when it runs out.

How do seats work?

Seats are for teams in FlutterFlow Studio. Pro can add a second seat and Max up to five; each plan above shows what a seat costs. Everyone on the team shares the plan’s AI usage in Studio. FlutterFlow comes with the plan’s main seat only.

Can I use FlutterFlow with a team?

Not in the same way as Studio. Only the team owner gets FlutterFlow. Seat users work in Studio, where they share the team’s AI usage.

Request team support
Does the Education plan include FlutterFlow?

No. Students and educators at colleges and universities can apply for a free year of FlutterFlow Studio. FlutterFlow isn’t included.

Apply for Education (opens in a new tab)
What happened to Lite credits?

They’re retired. Studio’s AI draws on the same allowance as FlutterFlow, so there’s one balance to watch.
